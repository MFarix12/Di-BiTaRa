## Arahan bersama

Create ONE polished wide landscape 16:9 background illustration for Di-BiTaRa, a Malay language learning web app for primary school children. Cohesive premium hand-painted cartoon airport/space adventure aesthetic, smooth bold navy outlines, softly shaded luminous blue forms, golden yellow trim, cyan highlights, clean white clouds, tiny sparkling stars. Colors navy #05265e, royal blue #0879e8, gold #ffd43b and white. Full bleed artwork only: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, signage writing, logo, watermark, user interface, buttons, cards or frames. Leave the middle 55 percent calm and uncluttered with a smooth mid-blue light field so real UI can be layered later; put most distinct scenery around left/right edges and lower quarter. Friendly, bright, spacious, visually coherent with a cheerful child pilot, rockets, airplanes and books brand, but no foreground people or characters that compete with UI.
