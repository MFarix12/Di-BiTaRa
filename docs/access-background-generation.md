# Latar akses Di-BiTaRa

Dijana menggunakan built-in image_gen, dua panggilan berasingan. Output PNG disalin ke projek tanpa memadam asal.

## Masukkan Kod

Fail: public/images/access-adventure-background.png

Prompt akhir:
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: landscape website background, 16:9, for the Di-BiTaRa primary-school adventure access-code page. Create one polished cheerful illustrated airport departure scene: luminous clear blue daytime sky, soft white fluffy clouds, a small white navy-and-gold airplane climbing across the upper side, welcoming terminal and a control tower near the side edges, subtle golden star accents and runway perspective toward a hopeful destination. Consistent high quality playful storybook game illustration, clean rounded forms, navy outlines, blue/navy, yellow/gold and white. Composition: deliberately calm open middle 45 percent for a large opaque form card; decorative airport details mainly left/right edges and bottom, readable when cover-cropped to portrait. Light, spacious, inviting, not crowded. Background artwork only; no lettering, no words, no logo, no UI, no frame or embedded card. Full bleed single continuous scene.

## Log Masuk Pentadbir

Fail: public/images/admin-control-background.png

Prompt akhir:
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: 16:9 landscape website background for Di-BiTaRa administrator login. Create a polished illustrated airport operations control center interior, clearly distinct from a children's outdoor departure page yet in the same visual family. Orderly modern navy control consoles at the lower left and far right edges, restrained blue glass screens with abstract runway diagrams (no text), wide panoramic windows overlooking terminal, runway and elegant control tower in cool late-afternoon light. Refined clean rounded storybook game illustration with navy outlines, blue/navy, white and modest gold trim. Professional, calm, organized, welcoming. Composition suits a two-column login page: leave broad quiet low-detail areas across the center, especially center-right behind the form; detailed architecture at edges, no visual clutter. Maintain recognizable operations-center atmosphere under portrait cover cropping. No people, lettering, words, logos, embedded forms, borders or interface mockup. Full bleed single continuous background artwork.

## Implementasi dan semakan

Latar khusus route pada body, cover, center, no-repeat, fixed dan minimum 100svh. Section borang telus; kad borang legap. Pentadbir mempunyai gradient navy lembut untuk keterbacaan tajuk putih. Preload imej mengikut route. Latar halaman lain tidak diubah.

Build berjaya. Semakan desktop 1280px dan telefon 390px: borang jelas, tiada limpahan mendatar. Bukti docs/screenshots/access-generated-background.png dan admin-generated-background.png serta versi -mobile.png.
